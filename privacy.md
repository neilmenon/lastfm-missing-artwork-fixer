# Chrome Extensions Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 21, 2026.

I respect and value your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how I handle and protect your personal information in relation to your use of my Google Chrome extension(s).

## Information Collection

My Last.fm Missing Artwork Fixer extension does not automatically collect any personal information from you. My extension includes an opt-in leaderboard feature that, if you explicitly enable it, will publish your Last.fm username, the number of artworks you've fixed using the extension, and the date of your last fix, to a database table I manage in AWS. I do not gather, store, or transmit any data that can be used to personally identify you, such as your name, email address, or location. None of your data is sold to third parties.
